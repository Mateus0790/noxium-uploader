Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Data accessed

With your permission, the tool uses the TikTok scopes user.info.basic (open ID, display name and avatar, to confirm which account is connected) and video.publish / video.upload (to post the videos you choose).

Storage

The access token is stored only on your own computer, in a local file. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

What we do not do

No analytics, no selling or sharing of data with third parties, and no access to your other TikTok content, messages or followers.

Deleting your data

Revoke the app in TikTok's settings and delete the local token file. No other copy exists.
